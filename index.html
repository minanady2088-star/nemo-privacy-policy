Official Privacy Policy for the Nemo Mobile Application
Last Updated: September 30, 2026

Welcome to Nemo ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to respecting and protecting your privacy while you use our Christian spiritual growth mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you use the Nemo app (the "App").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide when using the App, as well as limited technical information collected automatically:

A. Personal Information You Provide

  • Account Information: When you create an account or sign in (via Email or Google Sign-In), we receive your name, email address, profile photo, and unique user identifier.
  • User-Generated Content: Your personal and group spiritual tasks, subtask progress, spiritual plan enrollments, devotional notes, and group affiliations created within the App.
  • Customer Support: If you contact us via the "Contact Us" feature, we collect your message content and contact email.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device and Usage Information: Basic device parameters including device model, operating system version, app version, language preference, and anonymous performance logs to ensure system reliability.
  • Push Notification Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) registration tokens used solely to deliver scheduled devotional reminders and community updates.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To provide core spiritual growth tracking, plan progression, and subtask management.
  • To facilitate Christian community interaction and group activities.
  • To deliver automated, opt-in reminders for your scheduled daily prayers and plan milestones.
  • To provide user support and respond to technical issues.
  • To maintain security, prevent unauthorized access, and protect user integrity.

Note: We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data, nor do we use your devotional data for third-party advertising or profiling.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted infrastructure providers that adhere to rigorous data protection standards:

  • Google Firebase & Google Cloud Platform:
    • Firebase Authentication: User authentication and login security.
    • Cloud Firestore: Encrypted cloud database for syncing user tasks and plans.
    • Firebase Cloud Storage: Secure media storage for profile pictures.
    • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Timely delivery of push notifications.

    For more information, visit Google Privacy Policy.

4. Android System Permissions

The App requests only the necessary permissions required for its functionality:

Permission Purpose
Internet & Network State To synchronize tasks, spiritual plans, and group progress with secure cloud servers.
Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) To deliver daily spiritual plan reminders and group updates.
Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM) To schedule exact daily reminders for prayers, readings, and tasks.
Camera / Photo Gallery To allow you to choose and upload a profile picture or group image (only accessed upon user action).

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Account Deletion (Google Play Policy Compliance):

You have full control over your data. You may delete your account and all associated personal data directly from within the App at any time:

  1. Open Nemo.
  2. Go to Profile > Settings > Delete Account.
  3. Confirm the deletion request.

Upon confirmation, your personal account details, individual tasks, and authentication records are permanently erased from our active databases.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS) for data in transit and robust database access rules to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure.

7. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us immediately so that we can delete the information.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
