# Privacy Policy for Nemo

**Last Updated: September 30, 2026**

Welcome to **Nemo** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the **Nemo** mobile application (the "App").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By accessing or using the App, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by the terms of this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, as well as information collected automatically when you use the App:

### A. Personal Information You Provide
* **Account Information**: When you register or sign in (via email or Google Sign-In), we collect your name, email address, profile photo, and authentication identifiers.
* **User-Generated Content**: Tasks, subtasks, notes, spiritual plan progress, group memberships, and any details you create, assign, or complete within the App.
* **Support & Communications**: When you contact us through the "Contact Us" feature, we receive the message content and contact details you provide.

### B. Information Collected Automatically
* **Device and Usage Data**: Basic device information such as device model, operating system version, app version, language preference, and anonymous usage statistics to help us maintain and improve app performance.
* **Push Notification Tokens**: Device tokens provided by Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to deliver daily task reminders, plan notifications, and group updates.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:
* **Core Functionality**: To manage your account, save your spiritual plans, calculate task completion progress, and sync your data across sessions.
* **Community Features**: To allow you to join spiritual groups, view group members, and participate in shared plans.
* **Notifications & Reminders**: To send scheduled reminders for daily devotional tasks and community updates you have opted into.
* **Customer Support**: To respond to inquiries, feedback, and technical support requests.
* **Security & Reliability**: To detect, prevent, and address technical issues, unauthorized access, and security vulnerabilities.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services & Data Processors

We utilize trusted third-party service providers that adhere to industry-standard security and privacy practices:

* **Google Firebase / Google Cloud Platform**:
  * **Firebase Authentication**: Secure user login and identity verification.
  * **Cloud Firestore**: Encrypted cloud database for storing user plans, tasks, and group data.
  * **Firebase Cloud Storage**: Secure storage for user-uploaded avatars and plan media.
  * **Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)**: Secure delivery of push notifications.
  * *Firebase Privacy Policy*: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

We do **not** sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties or advertisers.

---

## 4. Permissions Requested by the App

The App may request the following device permissions solely to provide its features:

| Permission | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Internet & Network Access** | To sync your plans, tasks, and groups with secure cloud services. |
| **Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`)** | To deliver devotional reminders and group announcements. |
| **Exact Alarms (`SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM` / `USE_EXACT_ALARM`)** | To schedule precise local daily reminder alarms for tasks and prayer times. |
| **Photo Library / Camera Access** | To allow you to select a profile picture or group image (only accessed when you choose to upload). |

You can control or revoke these permissions at any time through your device's system settings.

---

## 5. Data Retention and Account Deletion

* **Data Retention**: We retain your personal information for as long as your account remains active or as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy.
* **Account Deletion (Google Play Compliance)**: You have the right to delete your account and all associated personal data at any time directly within the App:
  1. Open **Nemo**.
  2. Navigate to **Profile > Settings > Delete Account**.
  3. Confirm your request.
  Upon confirmation, your personal profile, personal tasks, and authentication credentials will be permanently removed from our active databases.

---

## 6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures, including SSL/TLS encryption for data in transit and encrypted storage at rest via Firebase, to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

Our App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us, and we will take immediate steps to remove such data.
